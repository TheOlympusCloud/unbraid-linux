=== The introduction film's sound
The film that opens unbraid's first start was made for unbraid. Its sound is mixed
from seven recordings on Freesound (https://freesound.org), each dedicated to the
public domain under Creative Commons 0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 asks for no credit; they
are listed here so the source of every sound is on record.

- 426767, "Apartment Room Tone.wav" by cMilan:
  https://freesound.org/people/cMilan/sounds/426767/
- 664929, "rope leathery creaking.wav" by vestibule-door:
  https://freesound.org/people/vestibule-door/sounds/664929/
- 596513, "Rope Tightening.wav" by EugeneEverett:
  https://freesound.org/people/EugeneEverett/sounds/596513/
- 624488, "Rope - leash - slide - tie - swoosh - movement - handle.wav" by ValentinPetiteau:
  https://freesound.org/people/ValentinPetiteau/sounds/624488/
- 792479, "Jewel - Heavy ring" by Vrymaa:
  https://freesound.org/people/Vrymaa/sounds/792479/
- 181889, "Whosh - Reversed/Reverbed breath" by PrdiChlp:
  https://freesound.org/people/PrdiChlp/sounds/181889/
- 141524, "Taylor Guitar Harmonics Pluck 01.wav" by BStateham:
  https://freesound.org/people/BStateham/sounds/141524/
